# Optional risk with a readable payoff

These are transferable design proposals, not universal rules or copied implementations.

## Purpose

Give the player a worthwhile optional commitment while preserving a clear main objective.

## Implementation

Provide a direct completion route and a detour with visible benefit and cost. State what can be lost. Bank reward at a distinct event; do not silently count unbanked loot as success.

## Tuning experiment

Vary detour length or reward independently. In lantern-vault optional gems add banked score but consume two extra fuel each; the player can choose routes avoiding them. Fuel and health limit collection.

## Failure modes

A reward on the only route is not optional. A dominated choice that is always worse is busywork. Hidden costs prevent informed decisions.

## Verification

Record completion rate, banked score and resource remainder for direct and greedy policies. Ask a human why they chose the detour; success metrics alone cannot establish enjoyment.
